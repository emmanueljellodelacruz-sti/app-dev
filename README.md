## My Favorite Series: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

> "Your next line is going to be..." - Joseph Joestar

**JoJo's Bizarre Adventure** is my favorite anime series. I like it because the fights are more about strategy and mind games instead of just random power-ups, and every part has a totally different setting and main character.

### All Parts in Order
1. Phantom Blood
2. Battle Tendency
3. Stardust Crusaders
4. Diamond is Unbreakable
5. Golden Wind
6. Stone Ocean
7. Steel Ball Run
8. JoJolion
9. The JOJOLands

### Why I Recommend It
* Cool character designs and art style
* Creative Stand abilities
* Good music and openings

More info on the [JoJo Wiki](https://jojowiki.com/)
